Task: Ant morphology (Design-Bench, continuous control).
Goal: optimize the morphology parameters of a simulated ant robot to maximize locomotion reward in a PyBullet environment.
Design space: 60 continuous variables describing limb sizes, angles, and physical parameters (bounded; evaluated via forward simulation).
Reward: higher is better; typical values are on the order of hundreds after environment-specific scaling.
This is a black-box optimization benchmark: the optimizer proposes designs and observes scalar rewards without analytic gradients of the simulator.
